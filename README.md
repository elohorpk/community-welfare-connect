
# Community Welfare Connect 🤝🏾

## 📌 Project Overview
**Community Welfare Connect** is a web‑based platform designed to bridge the gap between communities in need, NGOs, government agencies, donors and volunteers. It streamlines the process of requesting support, managing welfare programs, distributing resources and tracking impact — making social intervention faster, transparent and more effective.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Many communities struggle to access timely support because:
- Requests are manual and unorganized
- There is no central record or tracking
- Donors and agencies cannot easily identify genuine needs
- Impact reporting is difficult and slow

---

## ✅ Solution
This software platform allows:
✅ Individuals/Community Leaders: Submit requests for food, healthcare, education or empowerment support
✅ NGOs & Agencies: Verify requests, approve projects, allocate resources and monitor progress
✅ Donors: View projects, donate and see exactly how funds are used
✅ Administrators: Manage users, generate reports and ensure compliance
